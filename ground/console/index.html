Bench setup · Hall bypassSAFE AT THROTTLE 0 ONLY

For a rig without the Hall sensor: stores the bench throttle parameters (band 150 to 400, spin-up 300, Hall expect 200), then turns on bench mode, then the Hall bypass. It never arms. The bypass lasts until the box reboots.

Send 3 set_params? The parameters are written to the box's flash. With the bypass on, the wheel-speed interlocks are off and 400 is the only throttle ceiling.
