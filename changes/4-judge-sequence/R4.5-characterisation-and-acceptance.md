### Part A: G2, the plant (motor disarmed unless stated)

**Rig C.** The assembled box on its bearing and bolt, ESC power off at the bench supply, motor leads connected as in flight, XIAO on USB, `tools/capture-box.py --silent` recording. The ESC is off on purpose: a powered ESC at DShot 0 can brake the wheel, and any current in the windings would couple the wheel into the box and inflate the friction numbers (TD §10.1, A-17). No step in Part A except T-7 and T-8b arms the motor.

**T-2, breakaway friction.** String wound at 50 mm radius, add grams until the box turns (0.49 mN·m per gram). Five start angles 72 deg apart. Report the median and the worst. Pass: worst under 3 mN·m. Instrument check, from TD: a paper clip alone must not turn the box.

**T-3, box inertia.** The string goes over a pulley to a hanging mass m at radius r = 50 mm. The equation of motion is α (I + m r²) = m g r − τ_f, so with two masses the friction cancels:

I = [ (m₂ − m₁) g r − (α₂ m₂ − α₁ m₁) r² ] / (α₂ − α₁)

- **The wheel turns with the box.** With the ESC off, nothing drives the wheel, and whether its bearing drag and the motor's cogging carry it along at α is not known, so the wheel is made to: a strip of tape across the bell to the motor deck holds it to the box for T-3 and T-4. The fit therefore measures I = I_b + I_w, and I_b = I − I_w, with I_w = 6.50e-5 kg·m² (or R2.3's figure for the printed wheel). Check: `wheel_rpm` stays 0 (no Hall edges) through every run; a run with Hall edges is repeated. The tape comes off before anything arms the motor (T-7, T-8b), and the preflight turns the wheel by hand to prove it.

- Masses m₁ = 10 g and m₂ = 20 g, three runs each. α is the slope of `rate_dps` (converted to rad/s²) over the first 1.5 s after |rate| passes 10 deg/s, taken from the `--silent` capture, median of the three. A run with R² under 0.99 is repeated.
- Pass (TD): I_b within 30 % of 1.03e-3, so 0.72e-3 to 1.34e-3 kg·m².
- Instrument check: repeat with two 20 g masses taped 60 mm from the axis, opposite each other. The fitted I_b must rise by 2 m r² = 1.44e-4 kg·m², within 30 %. A fit that does not see an added inertia is not an instrument.

`tools/check-trials.py inertia --r-mm 50 --m1-g 10 --m2-g 20 --m1-runs a*.jsonl --m2-runs b*.jsonl` does the fit and prints α and R² per run and the fitted I. It does not subtract I_w, and its pass band is applied to I, so I_b and its band are worked out by hand in the log.

**T-4, running friction.** Spin the box to about 2 rad/s (115 deg/s) by hand, release, record the decay, five runs alternating direction, with the wheel still taped to the box. τ_f = (I_b + I_w) × |decel|, with I_b from T-3 and the slope fitted between 100 and 20 deg/s. Report whether the decay is linear (Coulomb) or exponential (viscous) by R² of each fit; the tool prints both. Pass (TD): under 2 mN·m. `tools/check-trials.py friction`.

**T-7, imbalance (arms the motor, Julian's go).** Box clamped, wheel at bias, `bench_mode` on, before and after balancing (TD §4.6).
- Firmware: R4.7's `accel_rms_g`, the only definition. With `bench_mode` true, `tlm` carries the AC RMS of the acceleration in the x-y plane (the plane an imbalance shakes; z is left out) over the last 200 ms (100 samples): each axis's window mean is removed, so gravity and mount tilt drop out, in g. With `bench_mode` false the field is absent, so the UI's payload does not change. Its host tests and mutant `rms_keeps_dc` are R4.7's.
- Pass (TD): below 0.05 g RMS after balancing, and the before value must be larger (balancing helped). Compare readings at one wheel speed only.
- Caveat recorded, not hidden (R4.7, FD §19.2): the quantity is measured after the accelerometer's 21 Hz filter (`DLPF_CFG` 4), which passes only 25 to 45 % of the 40 Hz bias-speed vibration (TD §6.4). So 0.05 g filtered is less strict than 0.05 g raw. T-8b is the binding vibration check; T-7 is the balancing evidence.
- Instrument check: tapping the deck must visibly raise `accel_rms_g`, and a bench-mode run with the wheel stopped reads well under the wheel-at-bias value. Exactly 0 g means the scaling is wrong.

**T-8, gyro bias and noise.** Box still, 2 min warm-up, then 60 s.
- Firmware: `gyro_raw` (MPU-6050 counts in the sensor's own axes, bench mode only) is already in `tlm` from R3.5; no change. Scale is 16.4 counts per deg/s at the ±2000 deg/s range.
- (a) Wheel stopped, SAFE. This is TD T-8. Split the 60 s into six 10 s blocks. Bias drift is the range of the block means; noise is the pooled RMS about each block's mean. Pass: drift under 0.5 deg/s and noise under 0.1 deg/s.
- (b) Wheel at bias in READY, box clamped (arms the motor, Julian's go). Same arithmetic on `gyro_raw`. This is what the rate loop actually sees. Decision [D]: if the noise RMS is above 0.3 deg/s (3x the TD no-wheel target, so 3 sigma stays inside R3.9's +-2 deg/s limit-cycle bound), the FD §11.1 notch filter is needed and R4.5 cannot close. A new feature R4.8 notch-filter (with its own spec; R4.6 and R4.7 are taken) is added to the set, and T-8b is re-run after it.
- Instrument check: the 60 s trace must not be constant, and a tap on the deck must be visible.

`tools/check-trials.py noise` and `vibration` do the arithmetic. Both refuse a capture that does not carry the bench fields.

**Decision rules, applied once T-2 to T-8 are in (G2).** Everything measured goes into TD §12.2 first.

| Measured | Action |
| --- | --- |
| I_b outside 30 % of 1.03e-3 | T-3 fails. Stop. Find the cause (a mass, the string radius, the gyro axis or scale) before any other decision. No gain changes. |
| I_b within 10 % | Constants stay. |
| I_b 10 % to 30 % off | Update `kBoxInertiaKgM2` in `controllers.hpp`, `i_b` in `plant_sim.hpp` and `docs/sizing.py` together. Set `k_omega` = 2π · 2 Hz · I_b and `k_i` = `k_omega` · 2π · 0.2 (FD §12.2). Rebuild, never add a wire parameter for inertia: the plant does not change at run time. |
| Wheel inertia (from R2.3's sizing) more than 10 % off 6.50e-5 | Same, for `kWheelInertiaKgM2` and `i_w`. |
| Running friction 2 to 3 mN·m | Inside the planning range. Record. |
| Running friction above 3 mN·m | Re-run the HT-5 and R4.3 simulations at the measured value. If PR-2 fails in simulation, add a feature for friction feed-forward. Do not retune on the hardware. |

After any change to a constant or gain, the whole host suite runs, then Part B runs on the final numbers.

**`sizing.py` re-run.** `docs/sizing.py` gets a `MEASURED` table at the top (I_b, running friction, anything else T-2 to T-4 produced), empty by default. When an entry is set, the script uses it and labels the output [M]. The script is re-run, `docs/sizing_output.txt` is regenerated, and TD §3 figures are updated from it (CONTEXT.md precedence: measurements, then `sizing.py`, then TD prose). Gate G2 is then signed by Julian in the log.

**Measurements become the defaults.** A box that has lost its NVS must still demo. Once Part A is done:
- The compiled defaults take the measured `ff_table` (T-5, 8 points, `ff_count` 8), `wheel_kp`, `wheel_ki`, `omega_low_rpm` (T-18), `spinup_throttle`, `manual_throttle_min` and `manual_throttle_max`, `hall_expect_throttle` (the feed-forward at Ω_low, FD §16), s_w, and `k_omega` and `k_i` if they changed.
- New default values need no `param_version` bump, because the stored layout does not change: a box with stored values keeps them, and only a box with nothing stored, or with invalid values, runs the new defaults. If this commit does change the layout, it bumps the version with a migration that keeps every stored value (decision record on PR 86: a version bump never resets a box; v2 to v3 is the example). The FD §16 defaults column ("0 until T-5") is updated to say "measured, R4.5".
- `rate_gain_scale` default moves from 0.2 to 1.0 only if both T-15 and T-16 pass at 1.0 and R3.9's sign check passed. R4.1 found that at 0.2 the host slews finish at 5.81 to 5.90 s against the 6.0 s timeout, so a reset box at 0.2 is one slow turn from a visible failure. If either trial run fails at 1.0, the default stays 0.2 and the cause goes in the log. FD §12.5 and §16 are amended to match.
- A new host test (`test_defaults.cpp`) builds the supervisor and controller from `Params{}` alone, with no `set_params`, and requires: arm to READY in at most 7 s, a 90 deg slew each way within 3 s at friction 0, 1 and 3 mN·m, and a 1 rev/s detumble in under 5 s. Falsifier: against today's defaults (`ff_count` 0) it must fail.

### Part B: acceptance, driven from the main UI

**Rig A.**
- The assembled box on the makeshift turntable, guard ring fitted, protractor disc fixed to the turntable base with a pointer on the box, a phone on a tripod filming the disc from above at 60 fps.
- Bench supply 10 V with a 2 A limit, or the NiMH pack if it exists (the log says which).
- The main UI from a laptop at `ws://direcsun.local/ws`, with R4.1's `tools/ui-turn-recorder.mjs` and `tools/check-ui-capture.py`.
- `tools/capture-box.py --silent --out` running in a second terminal. It is a read-only client, so it is independent of the UI.

**Preflight, every session:** the flashed commit is recorded; in READY, `tlm.flags` carries none of R4.6's `FF_TABLE_MISSING`, `RATE_GAIN_REDUCED` and `PARAMS_RESET` (so the table is loaded, `rate_gain_scale` is 1, and the box is not on reset defaults); `wheel_ref_rpm` is above 0 as a cross-check; the wheel turns freely by hand before arming (no tape left from T-3); battery above 9.6 V.

**B1, rehearsal, three times in a row, Aaron present for the first.** Pin confirm and arm; READY. Rotate right, then Rotate left, each ending TURN COMPLETE. Rotate right and press Stop mid-slew: the box stops and the event is READY/`mode`. Hand-spin and Detumble. Space-bar e-stop in SPINUP, READY, SLEW and DETUMBLE, one per rehearsal, rotating through them. Re-arm after the 5 s ramp-down refusal clears. Pass: every result is the one the UI's text promises, with no unexpected SAFE and no `HEARTBEAT_LOST`.

**B2, T-16 (PR-2).** Ten turns from the UI, alternating right and left, which is twenty 90 deg legs. FD §13.1 allows SLEW from READY only, so "from Hold" in TD T-16 is read as "from rest in READY at a zeroed heading".
- Scored from the silent capture by `tools/check-trials.py slew`. For each leg, t0 is the last READY frame's `t_ms` before the first SLEW frame (conservative by up to 20 ms). The leg's target is the commanded wire heading (+90, -90, or 0 for the return). `t_settle` is the last frame with |psi_deg − target| above 3 deg before the leg's window ends (the next SLEW, or 1.5 s after READY for the last). Pass: `t_settle` at most 3.0 s, an event SLEW/READY cause `slew_complete` (not `SLEW_TIMEOUT`), and |err| at most 3 deg throughout the hold.
- Physical reference, not only the firmware's own gyro heading: per turn, read the pointer on the video at three instants (before, mid-hold, after the return). The physical swing must be 90 +- 3 deg, the return must be 0 +- 3 deg of the start, and |psi_deg − physical| at each instant at most 3 deg. The reference is each turn's own start, because the UI zeroes the heading per turn.
- R4.1's `check-ui-capture.py` must also pass on the recorder's capture of the same run, so the UI path and the physical path are proved by one run.
- All twenty legs pass. One failure fails the run.

**B3, T-15 (PR-1).** The protocol of R4.4, with the main UI's Detumble button instead of the bench console, on the assembled box and the final gains: ladder trials at 0.25 and 0.5 rev/s, then ten valid trials, five each way. `tools/check-trials.py detumble`. R4.4's earlier ten trials do not substitute, because the wheel, gains or rig may have changed since.

**B4, summary and gates.** The session log carries one table of T-2, T-3, T-4, T-7, T-8, T-15 and T-16, and `tools/check-trials.py maxima` over every Part B capture gives the maximum `wheel_rpm` (must be under 4000) and the count of frames with a latched fault (must be 0 outside the planned e-stops). Julian signs G2 after Part A and G3 after B3 in the log. T-14 and G4 are not claimed.
