# R4.7: Send accel_rms_g in bench telemetry

**Status:** approved with changes by Julian, 2026-10-11 (decision record on PR 86): the x-y plane only, the 200 ms window, and the filter caveat. Implemented on the R4.1 stack
**Files:** `firmware/components/direcsun_core/{include/direcsun/core/estimator.hpp,include/direcsun/core/snapshot.hpp,src/estimator.cpp,src/protocol.cpp}`, `firmware/main/control_task.cpp`, `firmware/test/host/{test_accel_rms.cpp,test_protocol.cpp,CMakeLists.txt}`, `tools/{host-test.sh,mock-box.py}`, `docs/firmware-design.md` (§19.2), `protocol/{README.md,schema-v1.json}`

## Spec

**Problem.** The schema (`tlm.accel_rms_g`, bench mode only) and FD §19.2 define the field for T-7 (wheel imbalance), but the firmware never sends it: the bench block of `encode_tlm` carries only `gyro_raw`, `mag_raw`, `sun_a_mv` and `sun_b_mv`. The ground station parses the field and has nothing to show (issue 66, issue 80 P1.3).

**Approach.**
- `AccelRms` (core, pure, host-tested): a 100-sample window (200 ms at 500 Hz, exactly twelve turns of a 3600 rpm wheel). Each axis has its window mean removed, so gravity and tilt drop out. `rms_g()` is the square root of the mean total variance over **x and y**, in g: the plane an imbalance shakes, since the wheel spins about z. z is left out; it carries no imbalance force, only noise (decision of 2026-10-11, over #83's x and y over 1 s: the 200 ms window keeps the tick cost and already spans twelve turns).
- **Filter caveat.** The accelerometer runs behind its 21 Hz low-pass (`DLPF_CFG` 4, FD §11.1, TD §6.4), which passes only about 25 to 45 % of a 40 Hz vibration (2400 rpm). The figure is therefore attenuated, and T-7's 0.05 g target is judged on it: compare readings at one wheel speed, never across speeds, and do not read it as the true amplitude. It is a broadband figure, not the amplitude at the wheel frequency: the washer procedure of TD §4.6 compares readings at one wheel speed, which this serves. A per-frequency amplitude would need an FFT or a Goertzel at the Hall frequency, which stays with TD §4.6 step 5 (influence-coefficient balancing).
- The control task feeds every valid IMU sample into it and, on the 50 Hz snapshot cadence and only while `bench_mode` is true, copies `rms_g()` into the snapshot. Outside bench mode the field is absent from `tlm`, as the schema says, and nothing is computed.
- `encode_tlm` writes `"accel_rms_g"` with 4 decimals in the bench block.

**Not in this feature.** The balancing tool in the ground station (issue 66; the procedure in TD §4.6 and the TD §7.4 scope are for Julian to agree). Using the field for T-7 needs the wheel spinning, which is Julian's.

## Tests

- [x] Host (`test_accel_rms.cpp`): a still box at any tilt reads under 1e-4 g; 0.05 g at 60 Hz on x reads 0.05 over root two within 2 %; x and y add in quadrature; a vibration on z alone reads under 1e-4 g; a vibration that stops leaves the window after exactly 100 samples; fewer than two samples read 0.
  - Positive control: mutant `rms_keeps_dc` (the window mean is not removed) is caught by this test in `tools/host-test.sh --selftest`.
- [x] Host (`test_protocol.cpp`): the bench `tlm` carries `"accel_rms_g":0.0312` and validates against the schema (HT-9); a non-bench READY `tlm` with a measured value has no `accel_rms_g` field (asserted; the bench case shows the same check can see the field).
- [x] `tools/mock-box.py --selftest`: `accel_rms_g` is absent outside bench mode, present in it, and larger at speed than at rest.
- [x] `tools/check-all.sh` passes (9 of 9).
- [x] `tools/check-all.sh --firmware`: 10 of 10 on 3b3b0cf, run by Julian's agent; the firmware compiles with the `control_task.cpp` change and 0 warnings.
- [ ] Hardware, no motor: in bench mode in SAFE the figure is nonzero and under about 0.01 g (the MPU-6050's noise, inferred), and tapping the deck raises it visibly (TD T-7's own instrument check).
