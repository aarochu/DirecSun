# Bench checklist: what firmware each item needs, and how to drive it

Written 2026-10-11 against branch `p0/bring-fixes-to-r4.1` (also pushed as `feat/bench-ready-r4.1`, which CI builds). Every motor-capable step below needs Julian's explicit go, every time; nothing here arms anything. "On the branch" means the host tests cover it; none of it has run on the box.

**Flash this branch, not R3.7.** R3.7 (912aed8) has the battery divider and DShot300 fixes but not HOLD, SLEW, DETUMBLE, or the flags. This branch has all of them: V-67, V-68, R3.8 wheel loop, R3.9 rate loop, R4.1 ordering fix, R4.2 gyro heading, R4.3 HOLD and SLEW, R4.4 DETUMBLE, R4.6 status flags.

**Before any item.** The wheel loop needs the T-5 table: with none, `tlm.flags` shows `FF_TABLE_MISSING` and HOLD, SLEW, DETUMBLE and `wheel_step` are refused with `wheel loop not available`. The rate loop runs at `rate_gain_scale` 0.2 after every boot (`RATE_GAIN_REDUCED` in the flags): send `set_params {"rate_gain_scale":1}` in SAFE. `PARAMS_RESET` in the flags means the defaults are running, not your stored set. `set_params` is SAFE only, and only after the 5 s ramp-down has ended.

| 1 | Wheel speed loop follows steps | `wheel_step` (R3.8): ω_ref steps, rate and attitude loops suspended, PI tracks. Host: 2400 to 2700 settles within 10 % in under 0.5 s, overshoot under 20 % | SAFE: `set_params {"bench_mode":true}`. Arm, wait for READY. `{"cmd":"wheel_step","delta_rpm":300}` (±500 max per step, steps add). `mode READY` ends the hold. Read `wheel_ref_rpm` and `wheel_rpm` in `tlm`. The ground station has guarded wheel-step controls (#75) | None |
| 2 | Detumble | `mode DETUMBLE` from READY (R4.4): the READY zero-rate cascade. Host: the plant sim brings 1 rev/s under 5 deg/s | Arm, READY, spin the box by hand, `{"cmd":"mode","mode":"DETUMBLE"}`. Time to under 5 deg/s from `rate_dps`. Gyro range is ±2000 deg/s, so 360 deg/s does not saturate. No interlock watches box rate (only `TILT`: keep the spin flat) | None. READY already runs the same loop, so the mode adds a name, not authority |
| 3 | Hold, 60 s, three pokes | `mode HOLD` from READY (R4.3): holds the heading captured at entry, 1 deg deadband, relative to the last `zero_heading`. Host: a 10 mN·m, 100 ms poke returns within 2° in 0.32 s | Arm, READY, `zero_heading`, `mode HOLD`. Log `psi_deg` | **Risk, not a firmware gap.** The heading is the gyro integral. The bias is learned only in SAFE with the wheel under 200 rpm (τ 5 s), then frozen. Drift over 60 s = bias error x 60 s: staying under 2° needs the bias right to 0.03 deg/s. Sit still in SAFE for 30 s or more before arming, and watch `psi_deg` in READY first. An absolute heading needs R5.1 (spec only) |
| 4 | Slew 90° | `mode SLEW` (R4.3): 60 deg/s, 120 deg/s² trapezoid, `slew_complete` after 0.5 s within 3°. Host: all 60 cases settle in 2.36 s at worst, `slew_complete` by 3.01 s | `zero_heading`, then `{"cmd":"mode","mode":"SLEW","psi_ref_deg":90}`, or the UI's Rotate | None. The 3 s limit counts to the settle, not to the event |
| 5a | E-stop forces SAFE | Interlock `ESTOP`, any client, any state, DShot 0 at once then the 5 s ramp-down | Space bar in the UI, or `{"cmd":"estop"}` | None |
| 5b | Overspeed trip at 3900 rpm | `OVERSPEED` trips above `effective_cap_rpm + 100` (hard fault: throttle 0 that tick). The repo cap is 4000, so the trip is 4100. `bench_cap_rpm` (bench mode only, lowers the cap, never persisted) moves it: **`bench_cap_rpm` 3800 gives 3900** | SAFE: `set_params {"bench_mode":true}`, then `set_params {"bench_cap_rpm":3800}` (two commands: that is the tested order). The wheel loop clamps ω_ref to the cap, so a `wheel_step` can never reach the trip. Drive past it open loop: `set_params {"manual_throttle_max":N}` (up to 2047) where N is the T-5 throttle for about 4000 rpm, `mode MANUAL` input `throttle`, and raise the setpoint in steps (20 Hz max). Expect `OVERSPEED` in `flags` and SAFE | Permanent 3800/3900 limits are item P1.6 of issue 80 and wait for Julian's numbers (the code says 4000/4100). Use the bench override until then. It only lowers, so it cannot raise a limit |
| 6 | ESC behaviour on signal loss at 1500 rpm | Nothing in the firmware reacts to a pulled signal wire; this item observes the ESC. The firmware must hold 1500 rpm | Same setup as 1: from READY at the 2400 bias, `wheel_step -500`, then `-400` (1500; the floor is 900 and `UNDERSPEED` trips at 800). Or MANUAL `throttle` at the matching T-5 throttle. Prop guard on, then pull the wire. Record `wheel_rpm` from the last `tlm`, the ESC tone, and whether the wheel coasts or the ESC brakes | MANUAL `wheel` (R5.2, spec only) would set 1500 directly. It is not needed |
| 7 | Two clean demo run-throughs | The judge sequence: arm, wait about 6.4 s for READY, `zero_heading`, SLEW +90, hold 1 s, SLEW 0, left and right, Stop, e-stop | The UI's own controls | The R4.1 "UI against the mock" and hidden-tab items still need a run on a machine with a GPU. Pre-demo: table set, `rate_gain_scale` 1, no `FF_TABLE_MISSING`, `RATE_GAIN_REDUCED`, or `PARAMS_RESET` in the flags |

## What is not written, and why it is not needed here

- R5.1 (magnetometer heading, absolute HOLD), R5.2 (MANUAL wheel and rate), R5.3 (respin), R5.4 (SUN_POINT): specs only, awaiting Julian. None of the seven items needs them.
- The 3800/3900 limits as constants, and the 3.3 to 3.6 mm Hall gap, wheel inertia and bias in the printing notes (issue 80 P1.6): waiting for Julian to confirm the numbers.
