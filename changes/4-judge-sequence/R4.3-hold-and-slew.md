# R4.3: HOLD and relative SLEW

**Status:** in progress
**Files:** `firmware/components/direcsun_core/{include/direcsun/core/controllers.hpp,src/controllers.cpp,src/control.cpp,src/modes.cpp}`, `firmware/test/host/{test_slew.cpp,test_closed_loop.cpp}`, `protocol/` (only if the slew events need new causes; additive v1), `docs/firmware-design.md` (§12.4, §13.1, §23 rows)

## Spec

**Problem:** The judge sequence is a slew of ±90°, a 1 s hold, and a slew back. The firmware has the rate loop (R3.9) and a heading (R4.2), but no attitude loop, no HOLD, and no SLEW. The UI's own simulation uses the FD's limits, 60 deg/s and 120 deg/s², so the real box should feel the same as the simulation the judges have already seen.

**Approach:**
- **Attitude loop** (FD §12.1 step 1, §12.2): ω_cmd = K_ψ · wrap(ψ_ref − ψ̂), with K_ψ = 3.1 s⁻¹ and ω_cmd limited to ±90 deg/s, feeding R3.9's rate loop as its ω_cmd input.
- **HOLD.** ψ_ref = ψ̂ at entry, held until `mode READY`, `disarm`, `estop`, or an interlock.
- **SLEW** (FD §12.4):
  - A trapezoid in ψ_ref limited to 60 deg/s and 120 deg/s², along the shortest wrapped path to the target `psi_ref_deg`, which is relative to the last `zero_heading` under R4.2's amendment.
  - Complete when |ψ_ref − ψ̂| < 3° and |ω̂| < 5 deg/s for 0.5 s; this posts an event with cause `slew_complete` and goes to READY.
  - After 6 s it times out with `SLEW_TIMEOUT` and goes to READY.
- **The return leg** is a second SLEW to 0. Whether the UI sends the pair, or the firmware runs the whole sequence on one command, is R4.1's decision once the UI defines it. This feature provides both building blocks.
- **Plant simulation.** R3.9's `plant_sim.hpp` gains the attitude loop. Closed-loop host tests drive the real core code (supervisor, controllers, estimator) against the TD §3.1 plant with friction from 0 to 3 mN·m.

## Tests
- [x] HT-4: the trapezoid for 90° takes 2.0 s ± 1 tick (0.5 s accelerate, 1.0 s at 60 deg/s, 0.5 s decelerate). The shortest-path rule picks -90 over +270. A 180° target is handled deterministically.
- [x] HT-5 extension (sim): a 90° SLEW from rest settles within ±3° in under 3 s (PR-2) at friction 0, 1, and 3 mN·m, for 10 seeds of gyro noise. A HOLD hit by a 10 mN·m, 100 ms torque pulse returns within ±2° in under 2 s. Ω_ref never leaves [Ω_low, Ω_cap].
- [x] Positive control (sim): with K_ψ's sign flipped in a mutant build, the slew diverges (|ψ̂ − target| grows past 45°), and `tools/host-test.sh --selftest` proves it.
- [x] Timeout path (sim): a plant with friction raised to 30 mN·m, far above the planning range, cannot finish the slew. It posts `SLEW_TIMEOUT` at 6.0 s ± 1 tick and returns to READY.
- [ ] Hardware on the turntable, with Julian's go: 10 alternating ±90° SLEWs each settle within ±3° in under 3 s, measured from `psi_deg` and cross-checked against protractor marks. A HOLD poked by hand returns within ±2° outside the 2 s after each poke (T-16 and T-17 in relative form).
