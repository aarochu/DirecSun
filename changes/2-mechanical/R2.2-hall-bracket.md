# R2.2: Hall bracket facing the wheel's magnets

**Status:** planned
**Files:** `cad/hall_bracket.py`, `cad/motor_deck.py` (if the bracket's mounting moves), `cad/stack.py`, `cad/assembly.py`, `cad/out/*`, `docs/technical-design.md` (§4.4, §4.9, §5.3 A1104 row)

## Spec

**Problem:** TD §4.4 places the A1104 at an 18.5 mm radius, facing magnets glued to the 28 mm bell. The magnets now sit in the printed wheel (R2.1), at a different radius and height, so the bracket must face them across the TD's 2.5 mm gap (1.5 mm of air plus the 1 mm printed wall).

**Approach:**
- **Placement.** The A1104 sits under the wheel's magnet track, facing up at the magnets' south faces. It keeps the existing post-and-pocket design (branded face toward the magnet, behind a 1 mm wall), the slotted foot for ±4 mm of adjustment, and the M3 × 12 lock screw. The post height and radius are derived from R2.1's magnet pocket constants in `stack.py`, never typed by hand.
- **Clearance.** The bracket must not touch the wheel, the guard ring, or the motor leads' notch at −38°. `assembly.py` reports the magnet-face to sensor-face gap and fails if it is outside 2.0 to 3.0 mm.
- **Docs.** Update TD §4.4 and §4.9, and the A1104 row of §5.3, with the new geometry. The A-14 field-strength assumption is unchanged, because the gap is the same; T-6 still measures it.
