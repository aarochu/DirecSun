# R3.8: Wheel-speed loop and bench characterisation

**Status:** in progress
**Files:** `firmware/components/direcsun_core/{include/direcsun/core/controllers.hpp,src/controllers.cpp}`, `firmware/components/direcsun_core/src/control.cpp`, `firmware/test/host/test_controllers.cpp`, `ground/console/index.html` (bench panel), `docs/technical-design.md` (§12.2 records)

## Spec

**Replan 2026-10-10:** the main UI (`frontend/`) became the demo, so the MANUAL teleop input this feature first carried moves to Set 5 (R5.2). This feature keeps the loop, its tests, and the bench tooling that the UI's judge sequence (Set 4) depends on.

**Problem:** Open-loop throttle drifts with battery voltage and friction, so the wheel cannot hold a speed. The rate loop (R3.9) commands Ω_ref, which needs a wheel loop to track it. Gains and the feed-forward map must come from measurement (TD G2), not from the calculated plant.

**Approach:**
- **Requires the Hall sensor.** The wheel loop runs only when `hall_installed` is true. There is no bypass.
- **Wheel loop** (FD §12.1 step 4, §12.2, §12.3):
  - throttle = FF(Ω_ref) + PI(Ω_ref − `speed_fast`).
  - FF interpolates linearly on the 8-point `ff_table`, which must be monotonic.
  - The PI starts at 2 Hz bandwidth. It integrates conditionally: the integrator stops while the throttle is clamped in the direction of the error.
  - The output is clamped to 48..2047 while running.
  - Below Ω_low the PI is off and only the feed-forward runs. During SPINUP the PI closes once the Hall speed exceeds Ω_low (FD §13.3).
- **SPINUP, upgraded.** Ω_ref ramps toward `omega_bias_rpm` at 400 rpm/s through the wheel loop. Rung 1's open-loop SPINUP stays as the fallback while the Hall is bypassed.
- **Bench characterisation** (FD §19.2), with `bench_mode` on:
  - `wheel_step` (±500 rpm max) for T-5.
  - A temporarily lowered UNDERSPEED threshold for T-18, which is how the TD measures the sensorless low limit.
  - The console's bench panel runs the sweeps. The CSV download from R3.7 records them.
  - The results write `ff_table`, `wheel_kp`, `wheel_ki`, and `omega_low_rpm` through `set_params`, and are recorded in TD §12.2.
- **First spin on the real wheel.** The wheel's mass changes the loop. Gains tuned on the bare bell are re-checked once the flywheel from Set 2 is fitted.

## Tests
- [x] Host:
  - FF interpolation is monotonic and clamps at both ends.
  - The PI anti-windup holds the integrator during saturation and recovers within 1 s of desaturating.
  - The PI is off below Ω_low.
  - A wheel-only plant step from 2400 to 2700 rpm settles within 10% in under 0.5 s with less than 20% overshoot.
  - Positive control: a step large enough to saturate the throttle (800 to 3800 rpm) overshoots by under 10% with anti-windup, and fails that under-10% check in a test build with anti-windup disabled. If it did not fail, the test could not see windup at all. (Amended 2026-10-10: the original "past 30%" was an estimate; the measured mutant overshoot is 27.56%, because this plant tops out around 26 to 28%. The control's purpose, detecting windup, is unchanged; see Notes.)
- [ ] Hardware, with Julian present and his explicit go before arming:
  - T-5 map: throttle against steady Hall speed at 8 points, recorded in TD §12.2.
  - T-18: the lowest smooth speed is recorded, and `omega_low_rpm` is set 30% above it.
  - T-6: Hall rpm agrees with the scope phase frequency within 2% at three speeds.
- [ ] Hardware step response: `wheel_step` +300 rpm from 2400 and back, in `bench_mode`. Settling under 1 s, overshoot under 20%, no OVERSPEED, and no oscillation over a 10 s hold. Plots exported from the console CSV.
- [ ] Hardware limits: a `wheel_step` that would pass 4000 rpm is clamped to 4000, and the logged `wheel_rpm` never exceeds 4100. A deliberate overspeed test in `bench_mode`, with the cap temporarily stepped down to 2500 rpm through a test-only path, trips OVERSPEED and stops the wheel. This proves the interlock sees real Hall data.
