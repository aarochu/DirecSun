# R3.7: Rung 1, open-loop spin-up and MANUAL throttle

**Status:** in progress
**Files:** `firmware/components/direcsun_core/{include/direcsun/core/control.hpp,src/control.cpp}`, `firmware/main/control_task.cpp`, `firmware/test/host/test_control_throttle.cpp`, `ground/console/index.html` (teleop panel), `ground/link/direcsun-link.js` (only if the panel needs an API addition), `docs/technical-design.md` (§12.2 record)

## Spec

**Problem:** This is the demo floor (TD §11.4): the operator spins the wheel up and nudges it faster or slower, and the box turns the other way. It needs no closed loop, but it does need the whole safety path, and a measured answer to how fast the motor actually runs at a given throttle.

**Approach:**
- **SPINUP, open loop.**
  - Throttle ramps from 0 to `spinup_throttle` at 200 DShot units per second.
  - With the Hall installed, READY follows after 0.5 s within 5% of `omega_bias_rpm`. With the Hall bypassed, it follows after the ramp plus 0.5 s (R3.6).
  - In READY, until a later rung builds the rate loop, the throttle holds at `spinup_throttle`.
- **MANUAL `throttle` input** (FD §13.5). Rung 1 registers this input as built.
  - `{"cmd":"manual","throttle":n}` is clamped to [`manual_throttle_min`, `manual_throttle_max`], and also to `kBypassThrottleMax` while bypassed.
  - Staleness holds the last value; the 1 s heartbeat still applies.
  - Entering MANUAL starts from the current throttle, so there is no step.
- **Console teleop panel** (keyboard plus slider, as approved). It appears when the mode is MANUAL:
  - The up and down arrows send ±10 DShot units per press, rate-limited to the link's 20 Hz.
  - A slider covers the clamp band, and the box's own clamp numbers are shown.
  - Space is e-stop, as today.
  - Rolling 20 s plots of `wheel_rpm`, `rate_dps`, and `throttle`, plus a CSV download of every `tlm` frame (TD §7.4).
  - The arm flow first asks the operator to confirm the lock pin is in, or that the rig is clamped (FD §13.2).
- **Measured throttle-to-speed map.** With the Hall absent, motor speed comes from the oscilloscope on one motor phase. The electrical frequency f_e gives rpm = 60 · f_e / 7, where 7 is the pole-pair count (A-6; count the magnets to confirm). With the Hall present, Hall rpm is cross-checked against the scope; T-6 asks for agreement within 2%. The points go into TD §12.2 and set the real defaults for `spinup_throttle` and the manual band.
