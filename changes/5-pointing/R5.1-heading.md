## Spec

**Problem:** HOLD and SLEW run on a gyro-only heading (R4.2), so a gyro bias walks the held heading and the flag `HEADING_GYRO` says it is not a compass bearing. FD §11.3 specifies the complementary filter that fixes this, and the wire already carries `mag_axis_map`, `mag_cal`, and `mag_deg`.

**Approach:**
- **Filter** (FD §11.3): every tick ψ̂ integrates ω̂; on each fresh magnetometer sample ψ̂ ← ψ̂ + k · wrap(ψ_mag − ψ̂), k = 0.02 s / 2 s = 0.01 [A, tune in T-9].
- **ψ_mag**: the sample minus the hard-iron `offset` (per sensor axis), mapped to box axes by `mag_axis_map` (default `+x, -y, -z`), the horizontal (X, Y) pair through the soft-iron matrix, then ψ_mag = −atan2(Y, X). The minus sign makes a counter-clockwise turn of the box increase it (the field turns clockwise in the box frame).
- **No jumps under a reference.** The first sample after a calibration snaps ψ̂ to the compass, and only in SAFE. Armed, the heading stays gyro-only until the box is next in SAFE. A HOLD or SLEW reference therefore never moves under the loop. `zero_heading` still works as before: the reported heading is relative to it.
- **Flag.** `HEADING_GYRO` is set unless the heading includes the compass: calibrated, started, and a sample within 0.5 s. `mag_deg` is the absolute compass heading, or `null` until calibrated; it is not relative to `zero_heading`.
- **Parameters** (`param_version` 3): `mag_axis_map` (a signed permutation of x, y, z), `mag_cal` (`offset[3]`, `soft_iron` 2 × 2; refused if singular), persisted. Default is uncalibrated: the magnetometer does nothing until `mag_cal` is sent.
- **Calibration tool** `tools/calibrate.py mag`: records `mag_raw` while the box is turned by hand through a circle in SAFE, fits the offset (midpoint per axis) and the soft-iron matrix (the covariance whitening that makes the circle round), reports the roundness, and with `--apply` sends `set_params`. It refuses fewer than 150 samples, under 330° of coverage, or a line.
