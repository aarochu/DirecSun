# Test: A1104 Hall sensor and magnet, edge count by hand (R3.3)

- Date/time and timezone: 2026-10-10, 18:30 to 19:09 (America/New_York), four captures.
- Operators and witnesses: Julian (wiring, magnet, multimeter); Julian's Claude Code agent (build, flash, serial capture, analysis).
- Julian's explicit go for any motor-turning step: Not applicable. No ESC or motor was connected; the magnet was moved by hand.
- Component model, markings, and serial/lot: Allegro A1104 (unipolar Hall switch, open-drain output); one small permanent magnet (model and size not recorded); XIAO ESP32-S3 (MAC e0:72:a1:fc:49:5c).
- Firmware branch/commit and ESP32 target: `feat/set3/R3.3-hall-and-adc` at 872f624, the `firmware/test_apps/hall_adc` app, ESP-IDF v6.0, esp32s3. GPIO9 is an input with the internal pull-up on and a falling-edge interrupt. The app logs every edge, plus a status line every 200 ms with the ISR edge count.
- Procedure and acceptance-criterion reference: `changes/3-teleop/R3.3-hall-and-adc.md`, Tests, Hall item: each pass of the south pole adds exactly one edge, the north pole adds none (TD §4.6), and a spinning magnet gives a plausible rpm.
- Rig setup, wiring revision, guard, and stop method:
  - A1104 VCC from the XIAO's 5 V pin, OUT to D10 (GPIO9), on the breadboard.
  - Whether the planned external 12 kΩ pull-up to 3.3 V is fitted was not recorded. OUT idles at 3.27 V either way.
  - Nothing can move.
- Power source, set voltage, current limit, and measured voltage: laptop USB 5 V. Julian's meter: 5 V from A1104 VCC to GND, and 3.27 V at OUT with no magnet. (The meter showed both as negative because the leads were reversed.)
- Instruments, calibration or positive-control check:
  - Host arrival times from the raw serial reader ([`rawts.py`](2026-10-10-s3-stall-watchdog/rawts.py)).
  - The edge-capture path is shown able to see edges by the edges it did record.
  - Julian's multimeter on OUT, as an independent check of whether the sensor switches.

Also checked: D9. Julian's voltmeter read about 0 V on D9 throughout, with and without the magnet. A voltmeter on a floating pin reads 0, while a pin wired to the 3.27 V output would read 3.27 V at idle. So D9 has no wired path to the Hall output, at most a weak stray coupling that pulled the floating ADC reading down during some Hall lows.

## Outcome

- Overall: **Pass for the edge count** (steps 7 to 10). The rpm half is **not run**; Julian deferred it to the first flywheel spin, which needs his go.
- Faults, unexpected motion, heat, noise, or supply current limiting:

  - In the status line, `glitches` also counts restarts after a gap over 200 ms (as the FD specifies), so in a hand test it is mostly restarts. The `glitch:` log lines are the true rejects: 1 all session, during handling.
